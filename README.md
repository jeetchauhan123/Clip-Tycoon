# 📎 Clip Tycoon

**Build a tiny clip-making business into a thriving automated empire.**

Clip Tycoon is a business simulation game that starts with a simple
task---making clips---and grows into a balancing act of production,
pricing, employees, upgrades, and financial risk. Make smart decisions,
reinvest your profits, and see whether your business can thrive without
going bankrupt.

## 🎮 The Challenge

Every successful business starts small. In Clip Tycoon, you begin with
limited cash and wire. Make clips, sell them to customers, and use your
earnings to improve the business. As demand grows, you'll need to think
beyond clicking: how much should you charge, when should you buy
supplies, which employees are worth hiring, and how much automation can
you afford?

Your goal is to build an efficient operation while keeping expenses and
debt under control. Growth can be rewarding---but poor financial
decisions can put the entire business at risk.

## ✨ Features

-   **Produce and profit** --- Make clips manually and turn raw
    materials into sellable inventory.
-   **Manage your resources** --- Buy wire, monitor your stock, and
    account for production costs.
-   **Respond to the market** --- Adjust selling prices and balance
    customer demand against potential revenue.
-   **Expand your capacity** --- Upgrade your ability to purchase
    materials and transport finished clips.
-   **Build your team** --- Hire Clip Makers, Managers, Advertisers,
    Wire Buyers, and Clip Sellers.
-   **Automate the business** --- Let employees take over more of the
    production and sales cycle as you grow.
-   **Invest in upgrades** --- Improve production speed, output,
    purchasing, and marketing.
-   **Make financial decisions** --- Take loans, repay debt, and manage
    the consequences of falling behind.
-   **Choose your challenge** --- Start in Easy, Medium, or Hard mode.
-   **Work toward milestones** --- Unlock achievements and track your
    business statistics.
-   **Save your progress** --- Return to your game using browser-based
    save data.
-   **Play in your browser** --- A compact interface designed for
    desktop and mobile screens.

## 🔁 How to Play

1.  **Make clips** using the main production button.
2.  **Buy wire** when you need more raw materials.
3.  **Sell your inventory** in the market and earn money.
4.  **Set your price** to find a balance between revenue and customer
    demand.
5.  **Hire employees** to increase output and take over repetitive
    tasks.
6.  **Purchase upgrades** to improve the efficiency of your operation.
7.  **Manage your finances** by keeping an eye on wages, expenses, and
    debt.
8.  **Grow and automate** until your business can handle more of its own
    operations.

## 💰 Watch Your Finances

Money is more than a score. You need it to purchase materials, pay
production costs, hire employees, and invest in upgrades. Loans can help
you recover from a cash shortage, but debt can grow into a serious
threat if left unmanaged.

**The key is to grow sustainably:** earn revenue, control expenses, and
avoid taking on more debt than your business can handle.

## 🕹️ Difficulty Modes

Choose the level that fits your preferred play style:

-   **Easy** --- More forgiving starting conditions.
-   **Medium** --- A balanced challenge.
-   **Hard** --- Tighter resources and greater financial pressure.

Difficulty affects the conditions you start with and the challenge of
running the business.

## 🚀 Getting Started

This project is provided as a browser-based HTML game.

1.  Download or clone the repository.
2.  Open `Clip Tycoon (1).html` in a modern web browser.
3.  Choose a difficulty and start building your business.

No separate installation or build step is required for the standalone
HTML version.

> **Save data:** Progress is stored in your browser. Clearing the
> browser's site data or local storage may remove the saved game. Saves
> are browser-specific and are not automatically synced between devices.

## 🛠️ Project Focus

Clip Tycoon explores the design of an incremental business simulator
where several systems affect one another: production consumes resources,
pricing influences demand, employees add output and operating costs,
upgrades improve efficiency, and debt creates financial risk.

The aim is to make progression feel rewarding while keeping the player
involved in meaningful decisions---even as more of the business becomes
automated.
